Well architectured

1. Operational Excellence: Can I deploy, monitor, and roll back changes safely and automatically?
2. Security: Does every user and service have only the minimum access it needs, with data encrypted at rest and in transit? Who can access this, what can they do, and how would I know if they misused it?
3. Reliability: What fails, component or AZ, and what recovers it automatically?
4. Performance Efficiency: Am I using the right resource types for this workload, what is the bottleneck, and which service removes it?
5. Cost Optimization: Am I paying only for what I actually use, is there  a pricier option than the workload needs? and can I see who or what is driving the cost?
6. Sustainability: Can I achieve the same outcome with fewer resources, such as through serverless, right-sizing, or data lifecycle policies?
