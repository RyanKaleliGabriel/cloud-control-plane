Design one and make sure the following is ticked:
1. It is a secure architecture. Who can access this, what can they do, and how would I know if they misused it?
2. It is a resilient architecture. What fails, and what recovers it automatically?
3. It is a high performant architecture. What is the bottleneck, and which service removes it?
4. It is cost optimized. Am I paying for capacity I'm not using, or a pricier option than the workload needs?

Well architectured

1. Operational Excellence: Can I deploy, monitor, and roll back changes safely and automatically?
2. Security: Does every user and service have only the minimum access it needs, with data encrypted at rest and in transit?
3. Reliability: What happens when a component or an entire AZ fails, and have I tested recovery?
4. Performance Efficiency: Am I using the right resource types for this workload, and do I know where my bottlenecks are?
5. Cost Optimization: Am I paying only for what I actually use, and can I see who or what is driving the cost?
6. Sustainability: Can I achieve the same outcome with fewer resources, such as through serverless, right-sizing, or data lifecycle policies?
